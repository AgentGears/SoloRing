Disposition: v1.6 preserves the unchanged scenario

The v1.6 rerun preserves the original v1.0 pressure-test
requirements rather than rewriting them to obtain a PASS. The
review specifically confirms preservation of the load-bearing
scenario requirements:

  the Shot, and persist as fallen only through an explicit
  downstream handoff/adoption.
* Shot 25 still requires Eva to begin uninjured, acquire the injury
  at the authored intra-Shot time, and expose fresh as the later
  persistent state without marking the whole Shot injured at frame
  zero.
* chair-07 and other continuity-significant Production Instances
  still require durable identity/state subjects rather than
  display-name, hierarchy-path, or proximity retargeting.
* Composition publication still must preserve surviving occurrence
  identity.
* Composition Revision <-> SpatialWorldRevision agreement remains a
  mechanically provable binding requirement.
* Production Revision replacement remains compatibility-gated
  rather than silently substituting current/latest realization.
* Shot 51 still requires the same durable chair-07, its existing
  fallen state, the newer compatible chair realization, the new
  Composition, exact spatial binding, a new camera, and unchanged
  earlier captured history.
* Shot 58 remains explicitly blocked by the known
  Performance/contact capability gap; v1.6 does not falsely declare
  that capability implemented or solved.
* Shot 59 still resolves by earlier canonical narrative position
  rather than production order, edit order, or current/latest
  state.
* Candidate-world, simulation, reconstruction, and generated
  results remain candidate evidence until explicit adoption by the
  owning authority domain.
* An executor that cannot satisfy hard requirements still fails
  rather than weakening the Shot; a compatible alternative executor
  may create a new Generation without redefining the Shot.
* Historical Shot 22 still requires resolution solely from its
  exact captured historical graph, and missing required historical
  closure still fails closed.
* All 24 original adversarial negatives remain represented with
  fail-safe behavior.
* The original identity, temporal, authority, historical, and
  execution invariant families remain preserved.
* Later dialogue/performance, editorial/finishing, deterministic
  editorial-resolution, and QT cases are explicitly additive
  extensions rather than modifications of the v1.0 scenario.
